How the value is derived

Every number comes from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot. No external prices, no hand-picked names, no airdrop claims. The model calibrates itself on protocols in that same snapshot that do record a priced token.

What each factor means

          
    
            
  1. TVL is the protocol's own tracked total value locked. It is the base, never a guess about a future token.
    2. 
            
  2. Category ratio is the median market cap / TVL of protocols in the same category that record a priced token. With fewer than five peers the all-protocol median is used. Ratios are clamped to a sane band.
    3. 
            
  3. Growth factor rewards recent TVL inflows and discounts outflows: 1 + clamp(7d change / 100, −0.30, +0.30).
    4. 
            
  4. Age factor is higher for protocols tracked only recently, because a long tokenless runway tends to mean less upside left before a possible launch: clamp(1.2 − months / 60, 0.7, 1.2).
    5. 
            
  5. Chain factor gives a small, capped lift for breadth: 1 + 0.015 × min(chains, 8).
    6. 
            
  6. The product is rounded to whole dollars with a $1 floor. The detail view shows these six steps for every row.
